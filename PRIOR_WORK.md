# Prior work and scope of the search

The mathematical ingredients are credited in the paper and the imported formal
developments in [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md). The claimed project contribution
is their application and accompanying arguments establishing the full infinitude
statement of Erdős 345. It is not a claim to have invented those ingredients.

No earlier full solution was identified in the inspected sources. This is a
bounded search result, not a guarantee of world priority.

The official [SUMS 2019 program](https://www.jmu.edu/mathstat/sums/_files/sums_2019_v2_web-final.pdf)
lists the Haenel–Wood poster *Explicit Upper Bounds on the Anti-Waring Numbers*.
Its mathematical content was not recovered. The title alone cannot establish
whether its results overlap the present proof; this remains an unresolved lead.

The specifically cited `plby/lean-proofs/src/latest/ErdosProblems/Erdos345.lean`
was absent from the checked complete tree at revision
`8822f7ddef30fadbd92e1c6ab4ed897af356af5e` and the inspected path history. That
closes the particular source claim, not the possibility of a differently named
or unpublished result. The [public computational note](https://github.com/przchojecki/agentic-erdos/blob/c58f8589136880d44f4a4299e5fc01fd5084506f/notes/ep345.md)
inspected in full contains finite computations and an unfinished outline, not
the required infinitude proof.
